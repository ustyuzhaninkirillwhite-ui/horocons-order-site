An engineering team with experience since 2003

HOROCONS LLC does automation, commissioning and electrical installation of building systems. The company was registered in 2023, and its engineers have worked in the industry since 2003.

The team has worked in 13 cities across Russia — from Kaliningrad and Murmansk to Surgut and Khanty-Mansiysk: hospitals and medical centres, schools and a university campus, residential complexes, business and shopping centres.

We work with our own team of experienced engineers and installers and reinforce it on large projects to suit the scope of work. We assemble boards and automation cabinets in our own workshop — and install and start them ourselves.

Name

Why “Horocons”

The name comes from the Russian word “хор” (khor) — a choir. As in a choir or an orchestra: many people, and a conductor leads them so that everyone sounds together and in harmony.

It is the same on site: electrical, mechanical, low-voltage and automation systems must keep to one rhythm. One team is responsible for that harmony.
