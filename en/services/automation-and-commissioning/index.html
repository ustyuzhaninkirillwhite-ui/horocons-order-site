Scope of work

01 Systems

  • Heating substations (ITP, CTP)
  • Ventilation and air conditioning
  • Pumping stations
  • Dispatching and monitoring of building systems

02 Work

  • Site survey and technical specification
  • Programming of controllers and operator panels
  • Commissioning of automation cabinets and control panels
  • Individual testing and integrated trials
  • Bringing the systems to operating mode
  • Training of the operating staff

How we work

Stages of work

  1. 01
    SurveyWe study the design, the equipment and the readiness of the site for commissioning.
  2. 02
    ProgrammingWe write and test the programs of controllers and operator panels.
  3. 03
    Individual testingWe check every sensor, actuator and protection one by one.
  4. 04
    Integrated trialsWe start the whole system and bring it to its operating mode.
  5. 05
    HandoverWe hand over the documentation, train the operators and sign the acceptance certificates.

Documents after commissioning

  • Commissioning programme
  • Test reports and certificates
  • Operating mode charts
  • As-built diagrams
  • Backups of controller programs
