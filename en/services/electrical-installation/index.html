Electrical installation for building systems

We carry out electrical installation of building systems on large projects: in hospitals, schools, residential and business complexes. One team handles the electrics, the switchboards and the automation, so the systems are easier to connect and hand over.

  • Member of the Architectural Heritage Association (SRO)
  • Our own switchboard assembly workshop

What is included

Scope of work

01 Networks and equipment

  • Power and distribution networks
  • Installation and connection of switchboards
  • Connection of building equipment
  • Power supply of automation systems

02 Routes and lighting

  • Trays, ducts and penetrations through floors
  • Power and control cables
  • Working and emergency lighting
  • Earthing and equipotential bonding

Standards

  • PUE (Russian Electrical Installation Code)
  • SP 76.13330 “Electrical devices”
How we work

Stages of work

  1. 01
    DocumentationWe check the working design and calculate quantities and materials.
  2. 02
    PreparationWe agree the schedule with the builders and neighbouring contractors.
  3. 03
    InstallationWe lay the routes and networks, install and connect the switchboards.
  4. 04
    MeasurementsWe test the installed networks and issue the reports.
  5. 05
    HandoverWe hand over the as-built documentation and prepare the systems for commissioning.

Documents

  • As-built documentation
  • Certificates of inspection of concealed works
  • Cable log
  • Measurement reports
