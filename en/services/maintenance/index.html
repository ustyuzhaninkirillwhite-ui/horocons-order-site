Service and maintenance

We maintain systems after start-up — our own and those installed by other contractors. Large shopping and multifunctional complexes are currently under our maintenance.

  • Automation and fire systems under one contract
  • Fire safety equipment maintenance under an EMERCOM licence

What is included

Scope of work

01 What we maintain

  • Automation and dispatching systems
  • Heating substations and ventilation
  • Fire-fighting systems
  • Switchboards and control cabinets

02 What is included

  • Scheduled inspections according to the maintenance schedule
  • Checking sensors, actuators and protections
  • Backup of programs
  • Diagnostics and fault repair
  • Upgrades at the request of the operator

Systems of another contractor

  • First we survey them and hand over a list of remarks
  • Then we take them on for maintenance
How we work

Stages of work

  1. 01
    SurveyWe study the systems, their documentation and condition.
  2. 02
    ScheduleWe agree the list of work and its frequency.
  3. 03
    ContractWe fix the systems covered, response times and reporting.
  4. 04
    Scheduled visitsWe work to the schedule and keep a log.
  5. 05
    RequestsWe attend faults and upgrades.
